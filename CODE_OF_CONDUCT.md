# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) and the Islamic ethic of *adab* (good conduct).

## Our pledge
We want participation to be free of harassment for everyone, whatever their age, body, ability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, appearance, race, religion, school of thought (madhhab), or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour
- Be kind, patient and generous with your time and knowledge.
- Disagree respectfully. On questions of fiqh or interpretation, the app follows documented, mainstream sources and offers settings (calculation methods, madhab) rather than taking sides. Discussions stay technical.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Assume good intentions.

## Unacceptable behaviour
- Insults, takfir, sectarian attacks, or mockery of any faith or school.
- Harassment, sexualised language or imagery, doxxing, or threats.
- Spam or self-promotion unrelated to the project.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributions that violate this code, and may ban contributors temporarily or permanently. Report incidents privately to the maintainers via the repository's security/contact channel. All reports are handled confidentially.
